Automated Triage Workflow

How Mail AI processes, categorizes, and directs corporate email traffic

01

Ingestion & Preprocessing

Normalizes incoming email body & subject, tokenizes text, scrubs URLs and noise, and applies N-Gram TF-IDF representation.

02

Multi-Target ML Inference

TF-IDF features and Logistic Regression models predict business category, priority, and risk level.

03

Risk & Threat Heuristics

Evaluates phishing cues, credential harvesting keywords, unauthorized wire instructions, and overrides risk flags when necessary.

04

Department Routing & SLA

Routes to target team (IT Security, Tier 2 Support, Finance, Sales) with automated SLA tags and quarantine isolation for threats.

Enterprise Automated Triage Rules

Security Rule

Support Rule

Sales Rule

High-Value Inbound Leads

IF Category == Sales THEN Enrich company intelligence and dispatch Deal assignment to Enterprise AE under 4-hour SLA.
